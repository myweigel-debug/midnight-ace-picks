## Model summary
- **Market baseline.** Main lines are de-vigged from Pinnacle. Alt spreads, alt totals and alt team totals use the median of each book's two-sided de-vig at that exact line. `cg` = the other books' median raw implied probability minus DK's (BOL excluded).
- **Team model.** A recency-weighted (14-day half-life) ridge points model gives offense, defense and home court. Projections: GSW@MEM MEM by 6.4 (market 6.7), total 229.4 (225.5); LAC@SAS SAS by 13.8 (15.6), 227.0 (225.7); POR@MIL POR by 10.7 (10.4), 227.3 (229.0); NYK@MIN MIN by 3.2 (NYK by 0.5), 212.5 (216.9).
- **Blending.** The model gets 25% weight against the market, shifted on a normal curve (margin sd 13.5, total 18, team total 11.5) and capped at 6 points. Season residuals: margin sd 14.6 and total sd 17.9 against ESPN closing lines (n=222).
- **Availability shift.** GSW@MEM gets +1.5 points to Memphis. The Warriors are +9.1 per game with Curry and -15.6 in 5 games without him. Memphis is +17.2 in 4 games without Jerome, so his questionable tag doesn't hurt the Memphis side.
- **Props.** Projected minutes times recency-weighted per-minute rates, priced with a negative binomial fit to the Pinnacle (or consensus) main line. That fit put low rungs 3-7 points above DK, which is a fit artifact. Fair is therefore blended 50/50 with the backtest's measured cost for the leg's cost bucket (ladder -7.5% to -11%, -5/-6% when DK is cheaper; main lines -4%).
- **Leg cost (history/nba_backtest.md).** Alt spreads -5.3%, prop rebounds ladder -7.9%, team legs with `cg` -1 to +1: -6.0%. In expected cost the slate is about -11% (parlay 1) and -13% (parlay 2), before the model's availability edge. The availability edge is what lifts the parlays to the EVs below.
