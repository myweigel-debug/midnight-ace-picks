## Model summary
Same pipeline as earlier slates, rebuilt this run:
- **Market baseline:** Pinnacle no-vig where it has the exact market, else the median two-sided de-vig across `cs` books. Over-only ladders subtract half of each book's main-line overround.
- **Team legs:** shaded = 75% market + 15% empirical-residual sims centered on the Pinnacle line + 10% sims centered on a ridge off/def power model (30-day half-life, fitted home court 1.4). 40,000 bootstrap sims per game.
- **Props:** recency-weighted minutes x per-minute rates, negative binomial (var = mu + 0.09 mu^2), starters lose 14% of minutes in 15+ point margins; model weight 30%. Today's model ran about 4 points under the main lines on average (P(over) 0.46), so it leans against props.
- **Backtest blend (all legs):** fair = 50% shaded + 50% DK implied x (1 + backtest ROI for the leg's `cg` bucket), capped within 6 points of the market. Props at -150..-249 get the line-history shade (x 60.5/65.1).
- **Empirical check of the chosen cushions (two seasons of closing lines):** dogs of 5.5-7.5 covered +20.5 in 83.4% of 283 games (fair for SAC +20.5: 85.7%); dogs of 6.5-8.5 covered +21.5 in 86.6% of 277 (LAC fair 86.4%); dogs of 8.5-10.5 covered +23.5 in 88.1% of 210 (PHX fair 84.6%).
