## Model summary
- **Market baseline.** Pinnacle no-vig where it has the market; otherwise the median of each `cs` book's two-sided de-vig at the exact line (BetOnline excluded). For Over-only prop ladders, the baseline averages two estimates: a negative binomial fit to the de-vigged main line, and the median ladder price across books times the backtest hit/implied ratio for that stat.
- **Team model.** A recency-weighted (14-day half-life) ridge points model. **Walk-forward test (new today):** on 142 November games, each fit on prior data only, model RMSE was 15.4 against 14.5 for the closing spread, and every blend weight (10/25/40%) made the close worse. Model weight is now 10%, so team fair prices are essentially the sharp market. Availability adjustments: DET +3 and TOR team total -3.5 (Barrett and Murray-Boyles newly out), ATL +0.5, DEN +1 (Sengun and Adams questionable, Capela out), LAL -1 (Reaves), CLE -0.5.
- **Props.** Projected minutes times recency-weighted per-minute rates, priced with a negative binomial using each player's own dispersion, then shaded 30% toward the model. Across all 900 main prop lines the model is unbiased against the market (median projection minus line: points -0.7, rebounds -0.1, assists 0.0), so the big gaps are real disagreements. Most were explained by role news (Barnes's absences, Traore's DNP and bench start).
- **Backtest blend (new today, all legs):** fair = 50% model-shaded market + 50% (DK implied × (1 + backtest ROI for the leg's `cg` bucket)). From history/nba_backtest.md: team legs with `cg` +3..+5 cost +0.9% (the cheapest leg type), +2..+3 -2.3%, -1..+1 -6.0%; prop ladders cost -5% to -7.5%. Fair is still capped within 6 points of the no-vig market.
- **Monte Carlo:** 20,000 sims per game, with the margin and total drawn from the season's empirical residuals around the market (blowout share |margin| ≥ 15: 30-49%), plus a minutes- and script-linked rebound sim for Eason that mixes the Sengun in/out outcomes.
- **Cup check:** in 28 earlier Cup group games, favorites beat the line by 12+ points 17.9% of the time, against 19.7% in other games. That's no evidence of extra running up the score, so no adjustment.
- **Calibration (`nba/history/legs.csv`):** one night graded (4,061 rows). The team -400..-649 bucket ran 90.8% against 83.0% implied, but all 98 rungs came from 4 games, so no shading (see lessons).
