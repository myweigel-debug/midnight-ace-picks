## Model summary
Same pipeline as the 11-21 slate, rebuilt this run:
- **Market baseline:** Pinnacle no-vig where it has the exact rung. Otherwise, the median of each `cs` book's two-sided de-vig (BetOnline excluded).
- **Prop ladders:** the average of (a) a negative binomial (player's own dispersion) solved to Pinnacle's main-line no-vig, or the `cs` median when Pinnacle has none, and (b) the median ladder price × the backtest hit/implied ratio.
- **Team model (10% weight):** 20,000 sims per game from this season's empirical closing-line residuals (margin sd 14.2, total sd 18.1), centered on Pinnacle.
- **Props (30% weight):** recency-weighted minutes × per-minute rates, negative binomial, with a 14% starter minutes cut when the margin is 15+.
- **Backtest blend for all legs:** fair = 50% shaded market + 50% DK implied × (1 + ROI for the leg's `cg` bucket), capped within 6 points of the market.
- **Ridge power ratings** (pick'em blend, 10%): HCA +0.9; second-night back-to-back -1.5.
