## Model summary
The same pipeline as the earlier slates, rebuilt this run:
- **Market baseline:** Pinnacle no-vig where it has the exact market, else the median of each `cs` book's two-sided de-vig (BetOnline excluded). Over-only ladders are scaled by each book's main-line overround.
- **Team legs:** shaded = 75% market + 15% empirical-residual sims centered on the market + 10% sims centered on a ridge power model.
  - The ridge model weights recent games (30-day half-life), with HCA +0.9, home B2B -1.0 and away B2B capped at +2.0.
  - 40,000 smoothed-bootstrap sims per game.
- **Props:** recency-weighted minutes × per-minute rates. A negative binomial with a dispersion floor (var ≥ μ + 0.09μ²), because the raw fit was overconfident on ladders. Starters lose 14% of minutes in 15+ point margins. Model weight is 30%.
  - Bias check against the main lines: median μ minus line is +0.19 pts, -0.12 reb and -0.01 ast.
- **Backtest blend (all legs):** fair = 50% shaded + 50% DK implied × (1 + backtest ROI for the leg's `cg` bucket), capped within 6 points of the market.
- **Pick'ems:** 90% Pinnacle no-vig + 10% power model.
