## Model summary
- **Market baseline.** Pinnacle no-vig where it has the exact market. Otherwise, the median of each `cs` book's two-sided de-vig. For Over-only prop ladders, the baseline averages a negative binomial fit to the consensus de-vigged main line (using the player's own dispersion) and the median ladder price × the backtest hit/implied ratio for that stat.
- **Team model: 10% weight.** That weight follows the 11-20 walk-forward test. The empirical game-line distribution comes from this season's 235 closing spreads and totals: margin residual sd 14.3 with fat tails (P(fail by 20+) is 8.9% vs 8.1% normal), and total residual sd 17.9. It's smoothed with N(0,3) noise and blended at 10%.
- **Props.** Projected minutes × recency-weighted per-minute rates, then a negative binomial. Unbiased against the main lines (median projection minus line: points +0.1, rebounds +0.1, assists +0.1, PRA -0.2). Shaded 30% toward the model.
- **Backtest blend (all legs):** fair = 50% model-shaded market + 50% (DK implied × (1 + backtest ROI for the leg's `cg` bucket)), capped within 6 points of the no-vig market.
- **Monte Carlo (20,000 sims):** NYK-POR margin and total drawn from the empirical residuals, with Towns' minutes cut 14% when the margin hits 15+ (31% of sims), and points+assists drawn from his per-minute rate. P(20+) is 0.72-0.80 depending on the dispersion (his observed PA variance is below his mean). The published fair of 0.793 sits inside that range. Lopsided games (|resid| ≥ 15) finished 3.4 points under the total this season, which supports the Under in a SAS -18 game.
- **Back-to-backs:** second-night teams this season are -0.25 ATS (n=81), and 13.6% failed the spread by 14+ against 14% league-wide, so no adjustment for SAC or NOP.
