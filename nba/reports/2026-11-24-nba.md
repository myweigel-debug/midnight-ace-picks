## Model summary
The same pipeline as earlier slates, rebuilt this run:
- **Market baseline:** Pinnacle no-vig where it has the exact market, else the median of each `cs` book's two-sided de-vig (BetOnline excluded). Over-only ladders are scaled by each book's main-line overround.
- **Team legs:** shaded = 75% market + 15% empirical-residual sims centered on the market + 10% sims centered on a ridge power model.
  - The ridge model uses a 30-day half-life, with home court fitted from the data.
  - 40,000 bootstrap sims per game, drawing from 1,236 games of residuals this season and last.
- **Props:** recency-weighted minutes × per-minute rates. A negative binomial with var = μ + 0.09μ². Starters lose 14% of minutes in 15+ point margins. Model weight is 30%.
- **Backtest blend (all legs):** fair = 50% shaded + 50% DK implied × (1 + backtest ROI for the leg's `cg` bucket), capped within 6 points of the market.
- **New calibration shade:** props at -150..-249 get blended 50/50 with DK implied × 60.2/65.1. That bucket is 2.06 SE below implied over 404 player-games; see lessons.
- **Empirical check of the chosen legs (2025-26):**
  - Dogs of 9-12.5 covered +25.5 in 87.9% of 174 games; the model's fair at +26.5 is 86.6%.
  - Totals finished at least 18 under the close-plus-cushion in 83.1% of 978 games; the model's fair is 83.6%.
- **Pick'ems:** 90% Pinnacle no-vig + 10% power model.

## Why this parlay
**#1 ($50): Pistons-Magic Under 230.5 (-550) + Washington +26.5 (-660).**
- P = 0.722, payout 1.361x, P × payout 0.983, EV -1.7%.
- Summed backtest cost: about +1.8% (+0.9% per leg in the team cg 3-5 bucket).

It is the highest-probability pair that clears the 0.97 floor with both legs no shorter than -700. Both legs sit in the cheapest bucket the backtest found. They are in different games, which makes them independent. And they are the two leg types our line history rates best: Under rungs hit 84.5% against 84.0% implied, and dog cushions 91.3% against about 85%, over 36 games.
- Under 230.5 + WAS +25.5 is about 1 point less likely at a similar EV, so the parlay takes the extra point.

**Only one parlay:**
- With 3 games, a second 2-leg parlay must reuse a game. The week-6 rule (after 11-22, when one game sank both parlays) allows that only to reach the slate minimum, and section 1 sets the minimum at 1 for 2-3 games.
- There is no 3-leg parlay, since section 1 allows only 2-leg parlays for 2-3 games.

**Adversarial review:**
- **Under 230.5.** The loss path is overtime in a near pick'em (5.4% OT rate this season; Detroit's 284-point game on 11-12 went to two OTs) or a shootout. Detroit has had 7 games of 231+ and Orlando 5, but most of those came with closing totals of 216-236. Today's 212.5 reflects Banchero, Duren and Okorie being out, and both teams sit at or below their closing totals on average (DET +0.6, ORL -1.5). A Cunningham scratch would only help. The risk is real but already priced at 16-17%.
- **Washington +26.5.** The loss path is a Boston rout: Washington lost by 39 to OKC and 28 to Houston, failing the spread by 26.5 and 19.5. They failed by 16+ only 2 times in 18 games (league 12.6%), and the team-tail veto applies above about 3 in 15. Washington is at full rotation strength, and the power model has Boston only -6 (91.7% on the cushion), so the model leans our way.

**Stake:** EV is below -1%, so $50, the only parlay ($50 of the $100 allowed).

**Variety (4b):** 2 team legs and 0 players. There is no player leg because the 60% team-leg rule binds with only two legs; 4b says variety never overrides leg-cost rules. Week 6 so far is 3 team legs + 1 big. A guard and a wing are still needed later this week.
