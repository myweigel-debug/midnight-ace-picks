## What changes
1. **No game in two parlays** (11-22 lost both parlays to one game). Sharing a game is a last resort to reach the slate minimum, staked at $50 and flagged.
2. **Props:** only the one the variety rule asks for, `cg` >= +3, preferably rebounds/assists or a rung well below a stable starter's median. Props closed 4.6 points against us.
3. **Alt totals:** a higher bar (totals rungs trail implied by 8 points over 26 games); never two in one game.
4. **Stakes:** stay at $50 until a parlay clears EV >= -1%.
5. No bonus parlay proposal yet: the core parlays' expected profit is negative every day.
