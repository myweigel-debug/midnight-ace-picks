# Market backtest, NFL 2023-2025 (855 games)

Built 2026-11-17 from The Odds API historical odds. Snapshots: 15 minutes before kickoff (close) and Tuesday 18:00 UTC of game week. 10 books. Graded against ESPN box scores. Full tables are in `backtest_2023_2025.json`.
ROI is the return of a flat $1 single bet at the posted price. "Games" means distinct player-games (or team-games), which is the honest sample size, because rungs on one ladder move together.

## Tuesday vs close (DK, same rung)
Team markets (spreads, alt spreads, ML, totals): the Tuesday price equals the close on average (about 0.0 points). Props (pass/rec/rush yds ladders): Tuesday is 0.5-0.8 implied points better than the close and beats it 54-59% of the time. Betting early does not cost price.

## What it means for parlays
There is no free edge in any market. Profit has to come from picking better than the price. Each prop leg costs about 7-10%, and each team-market leg about 2-3%. A 4-leg all-prop parlay starts about 30% in the hole; a 4-leg team-market parlay starts about 12% down. Build mostly from team markets, and use props only with a specific reason and a good price.
