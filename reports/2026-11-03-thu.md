## Model

- **Market baseline:**
  - Pinnacle power de-vig wherever `px` has the same market and line.
  - Elsewhere, DK power de-vig.
  - Over-only player ladders: a gamma (yards) or negative binomial (counts) fit to the de-vigged Pinnacle main (weight 6), the DK main (weight 4), and every DK rung de-vigged with k=1.09.
- **Player model:** decayed (0.8/week) nflverse game logs give a model mean, priced with the market's fitted shape. The shade is 0.3 × (model − market), capped at ±4 points, and the **published `fair` is capped at market + 2.5 points**.
- **Team model:** ridge margin and offense/defense point ratings (0.9 weekly decay), HFA 1.5 (0 for Madrid), with QB and injury adjustments:
  - BAL −6
  - PHI −3 (Milton)
  - SEA −3 (Lock)
  - CLE −2
  - MIN −1.5
  - others ±1.5
  - Margin SD 13.5, total SD 10. Team legs are shaded 0.2 × (model − market), capped at ±4 points; pick'ems blend 80% market and 20% model.
- **Joint probabilities:** a Gaussian copula with 400k draws. Same-game legs (JAX ML + Starks) use rho 0.05; cross-game legs are independent. The app's 0.8 same-game profit trim is applied to payouts.
- **Sensitivity:** under conservative multiplicative de-vig (rung imp/1.05), the joints are **0.64, 0.64 and 0.52**, still above the 0.55 and 0.42 targets.
