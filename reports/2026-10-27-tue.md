## Why these won out
For each required size, an optimizer searched every combination of legs from distinct games (so there is no same-game 20% trim and the legs are independent). It took the 5-leg first, then the 3-leg and 2-leg from the remaining legs, so no leg repeats and the slate stays diversified. Combinations that went negative under the 3-point haircut were rejected. Main sides and totals were mostly within a point of the model after blending, so the edges came from player usage and stat-distribution mispricing rather than sides. The exception is MIN +3, where the model has Minnesota favored. Alt-ladder legs (Maye 15+ rush yds, Higgins 80+, Washington 25+, Hill 5+) beat the main lines because those rungs sit where the player's floor is well above the line. Higgins 90+ (+221) priced slightly better than 80+, but the 80+ rung was kept to stay away from the book's tail shading and because of the Cincinnati wind.

**Uncertainty check:** a Monte Carlo drew each leg's fair probability from a Beta distribution (effective n=40). Share of draws with positive EV: 5-leg 95%, 3-leg 77%, 2-leg 72%. Simulated hit rates match `prob`.

**Final price check:** before publishing, the board and the event groups behind every chosen leg were re-fetched at the 20-minute mark. Every published price matches a fetched response.
