## Model summary (same method as Tuesday, the post-Week-11 fix)
- **Market baseline:** proportional de-vig. Use Pinnacle when it posts both sides at the exact line; otherwise use the median across the `cs` books (excluding DK and BetOnline) that post both sides. Over-only ladders: each book's rung price divided by its main-line overround, then the median. `cg` = the other books' median raw implied minus DK's implied (+ = DK cheaper).
- **Team sides:** empirical margin distribution from games.csv (2012+, closing spread within ±1, or ±3 shifted for spreads of 8+, and closing total within ±3.5). Shade = 25% of (history - market), capped at ±1.5 pts. Power shade: opponent-adjusted, recency-weighted (0.9/week) EPA/play ridge ratings from 2026 play-by-play, scaled to the board (market margin = 72.5 x EPA diff + 1.5). The shade is 25% of the model-market gap, capped at 1 pt of spread, then halved. PIT games get half again (QB change).
- **Totals:** history shade only, and only in domes/closed roofs or benign weather (Thursday rule). That excludes BUF, PIT, CIN, SF, CLE and IND (outdoor rain; the IND roof should be closed, but I left it out to be safe).
- **Props:** proportional consensus, a -2 pt haircut on Overs, and a season hit-rate shade (0.3 x (smoothed hit rate - market), capped -3/+1.5). Thursday role filter: QBs with no injury designation and a settled starting job; every-down lead backs; WR1/TE1 at 85%+ snaps for 3 weeks; nobody on this week's injury report.
- **Same-game check:** I measured the joint hit rate of alt spread + alt total pairs empirically from games.csv for every game. The best same-game pair reached P x payout 0.93 after the app's 20% trim, so every leg is in a different game and the joint probability is the product of the legs.
- **Monte Carlo:** 20,000 bootstrap draws per game from similar historical games, shifted to the market spread and total. Chosen legs: CAR +8.5 0.731 (n=803), JAX -7.5 0.832 (n=58, a small sample, so I trust Pinnacle's 0.701), DAL/PHI U56.5 0.721 (n=322); NE +8.5 0.810 (n=80) before the swap, which implies LAC +7.5 is roughly at or above the 0.736 fair at the new NE -2.5 line. The sims are at or above the fairs I used, so the fairs are not optimistic.

## Why these two parlays
I searched every 2- and 3-leg combination of the eligible legs (102 legs within 5% of break-even, every leg in a different game, at most one prop) for the highest P(hit) with P x payout ≥ 0.97.
- Only 4 pairs clear 0.97, and all four use Panthers +8.5. No 3-leg combination clears it (best ~0.95).
- **Parlay 1** pairs Panthers +8.5 with Jaguars -7.5 (P 0.513, P x payout 0.972 at the re-poll). The alternative Panthers + Dolphins +8.5 (0.972) has a lower P (0.474), and the whole market has been moving away from Miami.
- **Parlay 2** is the best pair without CAR@TB or TEN@JAX. The first build was Patriots +8.5 with the Eagles-Cowboys Under 56.5 (P 0.561, 0.947). At the 19:58Z re-poll, DK moved Patriots +8.5 from -429 to -515 (0.924) and left the Chargers side cheap, so Chargers +7.5 (-295, Pinnacle 0.739) with the Under is now the best pair (P 0.524, 0.960). Re-ranked with fresh prices, Chargers +7.5 + Panthers +8.5 (0.977) would beat parlay 1, but Jaguars -7.5 + the Under (about 0.952) would then be the weak parlay. Splitting them as published gives two parlays near the floor (0.972 / 0.960) instead of 0.977 / 0.952, with the same total EV.
- The three-parlay version (2+2+3) is impossible: no 3-leg parlay reaches 0.95, let alone 0.97.
