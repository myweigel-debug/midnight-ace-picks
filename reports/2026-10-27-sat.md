## Model
- **Team:** ridge margin ratings (0.9 weekly decay), an off/def points model, and pbp EPA/play. The model margin is 0.5 x ridge(x1.6) + 0.5 x EPA, plus 1.5 HFA and injury/trade adjustments (Lamar back, Simmons/Nelson/Buckner trades, Cross, Derwin James, NE's A.J. Brown and Henderson, TB storms and Egbuka). The blend is 75% market margin and 80% market total. A drive-level Monte Carlo (40k sims per game) puts TD and FG rates on each drive from team red-zone/FG tendencies and kicker make rates, giving integer scores with key numbers. Margin SD is about 13.5 and total SD about 13.5–14.5. Alt team lines are only priced within ±4–5 points of the projection, because the sim's tails run fatter than DraftKings'.
- **Kickers:** the same sim provides FGA, FGM, PATs and kicking points jointly. Raw sim P(over) ran 4 points high against the de-vigged market across all 26 kickers, so it is de-biased and then blended 50/50 with the market.
- **Props:** ESPN game logs, zero-filled from nflverse snaps, decay 0.85. Opponent factors cover stat allowed, plays and run rate for tackles, and sacks allowed. Script factors come from the implied team total and margin, and each family has its median model/market bias removed. Market mean and shape (gamma, NB, normal or Poisson) are fitted to DK's main line plus its alt ladder. The final mean is 60% market and 40% model; the published fair = market + 0.6 x (model − market).
- **Filters:** at least 4 games, model/market mean ratio 0.72–1.38, snap share not falling, odds from -300 to +250, EV >= 4% for main lines and 6% for alt rungs, and a sensitivity check (mean shifted 5% against the bet must keep EV > 0).
- **Parlays:** legs are sampled jointly from the game sims. Team and kicker legs use sim outcomes directly; props use a Gaussian copula on team points or margin (rho 0.15–0.35). The app's 20% same-game profit trim is applied. A simulated-annealing optimizer maximized the sum of parlay EVs under a 2-point haircut on every leg, and I then hand-adjusted for news and diversity. All 7 parlays stay +EV after the haircut.
