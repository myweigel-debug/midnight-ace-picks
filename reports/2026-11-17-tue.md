## Model summary
- **Team lines.** Each game's margin, total and team-score distributions come from unsmoothed 2015+ closing-line results (games.csv) for games with similar lines; integer shifts only, so key numbers stay intact. These are anchored to the Pinnacle main line and corrected toward Pinnacle's no-vig price at every posted alt point (corrections were within ±5 pts).
  - **Consensus check (new):** at every DK rung, I take the median *power* de-vig across books that post both sides. For alt spreads and alt team totals with 2+ consensus books, the market probability is 50% model + 50% consensus. The proportional de-vig I tried first ran 3-7 pts low at short prices; power de-vig agrees with the model within ~2 pts.
  - **Power shade:** opponent-adjusted, recency-weighted EPA/play ratings (Week 10 data, regressed 50%), home field 1.5 (0 for Mexico City). The shade is 25% of the model-minus-market gap (12.5% when a team changed QBs), capped at ±1 pt and converted to probability with the local empirical density. TEN +20.5 gets half of that.
- **Props.** I fit a normal (QB yards), gamma with a small dud-game mass (rush/rec yards) or negative binomial (counts) jointly to the no-vig main line (Pinnacle, else the median power de-vig across books) and DK's ladder, with a free power-vig exponent. The market probability at each rung is 50% that fit + 50% the median per-book power de-vig at the exact rung (−0.5 pt).
  - Shade: 0.3 × (smoothed season hit rate − market), capped at −3/+1.5 pts.
  - **Calibration haircut −1.5 pts** on every prop Over (line-history rule; see lessons.md).
- **Parlays.** Every leg is in a different game, so the joint probability is the product of the legs and there is no same-game trim. No leg is reused across parlays.
- **Sensitivity.** P × payout for the three parlays is [0.979, 0.971, 0.971] as published. With no prop haircut: [0.978→0.996, 0.990, 0.990]. With the full 3-pt measured gap, all three fall to 0.95-0.96 and would fail the floor. Dropping TEN's shade entirely takes parlay 2 to 0.958. This slate is close to break-even and depends on the haircut call, which is why every stake is $50.
