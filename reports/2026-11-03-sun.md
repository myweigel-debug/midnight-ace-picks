## Model

- **Market baseline for team lines:**
  - Pinnacle power de-vig for mains and Pinnacle's two-sided alt lines.
  - A normal margin and total fit jointly to Pinnacle's main, Pinnacle's alts, DK's main and the DK alt ladder (rungs de-vigged at /1.03). The fitted margin SD was about 11-12 and the total SD about 11-12.5.
  - Team totals: a normal fit to the team-total mains and ladder, anchored at (total ± margin)/2.
- **Player props:**
  - Gamma distributions for yards; negative binomial for counts (receptions, attempts, carries, tackles, FGs, PATs, kicking points).
  - Mean and dispersion are fit jointly to the Pinnacle main (weight 6), the DK main (weight 4) and every DK ladder rung (weight 1). The prior pulls the fit toward a dispersion pooled over starters.
  - The alt-rung market probability is the average of that fit and the rung de-vigged on its own (imp/1.03). Kickers take the lower of the two.
- **Model shade:**
  - Players: decay-weighted (0.8/week) 2026 logs give a model mean, blended 35/65 with the market mean. The shade is 0.3 × (model − market), capped at ±4 points.
  - Team legs: ridge power ratings with 0.9 weekly decay, plus QB/injury adjustments (SEA −3, PHI −3, MIN −1.5, CLE −1.5, GB −1.5, others ±1). The shade is 0.2 × the difference.
  - Published `fair` is capped at market + 2.5 points.
- **Joint probability:** all six parlays use legs from different games, so legs are independent. Monte Carlo with 200k draws per parlay matched the analytic product. The app's 0.8 same-game trim never applies.
- **Sensitivity:** under a conservative de-vig (each leg at the lower of market and imp/1.045) the joints are 0.73, 0.73, 0.72, 0.73, 0.62, 0.61. With a stricter rung de-vig (imp/1.06) they are about 0.71, 0.71, 0.70, 0.71, 0.59 and 0.60. All are well above the 0.55 and 0.42 targets.
