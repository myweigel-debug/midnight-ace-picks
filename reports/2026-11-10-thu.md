## Model summary
- **Props.**
  - **Joint fit.** I fit each player's distribution jointly to:
    - the Pinnacle no-vig main (weight 3);
    - every  book's no-vig main;
    - every book's alt ladder, each with its own vig offset.
  - **Distribution.** I used a gamma distribution for yards and a negative binomial for counts, on a mean x shape grid.
  - **Dud-game allowance (new).** A small zero-inflation term covers receiving and rushing. Leave-one-out tests on 2026 logs showed that the pure gamma overstated very low rungs by 3-6 pts.
  - **Shading.** I blended 60% market mean with 40% recency-weighted log mean x opponent factor (regressed 50%). The shade is capped at +2.5 / -6 pts.
  - **Consensus check.** DK's price had to be at or better than the raw median of other books at the same rung.
- **Team lines.**
  - Alt spreads use the empirical distribution of final margins for games with a closing spread within ±1 pt (2012+), with no smoothing across key numbers. That distribution is centered and corrected to Pinnacle's 26-point ladder.
  - Totals and team totals use the same windowed approach.
  - My first version smoothed with a kernel and faked 3-4% edges on +1.5/+2.5 rungs. I caught and fixed this; see lessons.md.
- **Power ratings:** recency-weighted, opponent-adjusted EPA/play, used for pick'ems only (20% weight, 80% market).
- **Parlays:** every leg is in a different game, so P(hit) is the product of the leg probabilities. The app payout is the product of decimal odds, with no same-game trims.
