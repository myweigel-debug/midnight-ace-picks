## Model summary
- **Market baseline:** for two-sided markets, Pinnacle's power de-vig when Pinnacle posts the market; otherwise the median of each book's power de-vig (BetOnline excluded). For Over-only ladders, each book's rung is de-vigged with the exponent from that book's own two-sided main line, then I take the median and subtract 0.5 pt for ladder hold. `cg` = the other books' median implied minus DK implied, at the same rung.
- **Team legs:** alt spreads, alt totals and team totals come from the empirical 2015+ games.csv distribution of results around the same closing line. For spreads under 8, that is games within ±1 pt, unshifted, so the key numbers stay intact. For larger spreads, it is games within ±3, shifted by whole points. I blend that 50/50 with the market. The power ratings are 2026 EPA/play net (offense minus defense) × 62 plays, plus 1.5 for home. They feed in at 25% of the gap to the market, capped at 1 pt of spread and 2.5 prob pts per leg, and are switched off for teams with QB changes (ARI, PHI, BAL, MIA, MIN, NYJ, LV, TB).
- **Moneylines:** Pinnacle no-vig, +1 pt for favorites above 75%. The games.csv check (2010-17 vs 2018-25) has >75% favorites at +1.1/+1.4 pts and 70-80% favorites at +0.9/+2.5. Divisional games (+0.4/-0.5) didn't replicate. Favorites in 15+ mph wind ran -2.1/-0.8. The power shade on an ML is dropped when the historical sim at that spread and total disagrees by more than 2 pts. That removed Dallas's +1.6.
- **Joint sims:** 10,000 draws per game, resampling (margin, total) pairs from 2015+ games with similar closing spreads and totals, shifted to today's lines. They cross-check every team leg (table below). No parlay has two legs from the same game, so the joint probability is the product of the legs and the app's same-game trim never applies.
- **Props:** the market baseline plus a small relative shade from 2026 logs (last 8 games, beta prior on the market with weight 6). The shade is 0.3 × (model − market − family median bias), capped at -3/+1.5 pts. Overs also get the **-1.5 pt calibration haircut** from the Week 10 line history, and -1 pt more if the player missed the rung in 2+ of his last 6 games. Published fair stays within 6 pts of the market (in practice within 2.5).
- **Sensitivity:** with a full -3 pt prop haircut, every core parlay still clears the 0.97 floor (lowest: sun-2 at 0.976, sun-6 at 0.988) and the bonuses stay above -6% EV.
- **Defense and kicker legs:** none qualified. Every tackle and kicker rung was priced by at most 2 books in `cs`, and the 4b bar needs 3.
