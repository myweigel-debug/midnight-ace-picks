## Model
- **Team:** ridge power ratings on margin (HFA 1.5, 0.9 weekly decay) plus decayed points for and against. The projection is 75% market margin and 80% market total. Errors are normal with SD 13.5 (margin), 10 (total) and 8.5 (team total). No team-side leg cleared the bar once Philadelphia's unclear QB situation was taken into account. ESPN's PHI injury list shows Tanner McKee and Cole Payton but not Jalen Hurts, and I could not confirm the starter, so the season-long ratings were not trusted there.
- **Props:** decay-weighted (0.87) logs with opponent-allowed and implied-team-total adjustments. The mean blends 40% model with 60% the market mean implied by the de-vigged main line. New this run: the distribution shape (gamma CV or negative-binomial dispersion) is fitted to DraftKings' own alt ladder for each player, which removes the false long-shot alt edges produced by a fixed-CV tail.
- **Filters:** odds between -320 and +220, at least 5 logged games, the player Active, sensitivity EV > 0 (mean shifted 8% against the bet), and an nflverse last-5 hit rate within 15 points of fair. Each final fair is the model fair minus a 1.5-8 point haircut. Each parlay keeps positive EV after a further 3-point haircut on every leg.
- **Rejected after review:** Michael Penix Jr. U0.5 INT. Tampa Bay forces 1.14 INT/g (league 0.69), so the edge vanished once the opponent was adjusted. Justin Jefferson U5.5 receptions: an ESPN name collision with a Browns player of the same name was deflating his mean. QB pass-yard Unders (Herbert, Nix, Ward, Mayfield): the model ran systematically about 25% under market, so they were treated as bias, not edge. Zay Flowers: Lamar Jackson is Questionable. The 2-leg pairs the highest-confidence leg (Gordon) with the best plus-money rung (Higgins 80+). The 3-leg uses three legs at -107 to -117 with 11-16% EV.
