## Model
- **Market baseline:** two-way markets use power de-vig. This covers main lines, every alt spread, alt total and team-total rung (each paired with its mirror rung), and halves. Power de-vig suits lopsided prices because it puts most of the hold on the longshot side. Without Pinnacle, this is the baseline.
- **Player alt ladders (Over only):** I fit a gamma distribution (yards) or a negative binomial (counts) to the de-vigged main line (weight 4) plus every ladder rung. The rungs are de-vigged with the median power exponent from main lines, k = 1.09. Each rung's market probability is read off the fitted distribution.
- **Team model:** ridge margin ratings (0.9 weekly decay) are combined 50/50 with nflverse EPA/play ratings. HFA is 1.5, or 0 for Madrid. Injury/QB adjustments:
  - BAL -7 (Lamar on IR, Huntley out, rookie Fagnano)
  - PHI -1.5 (Hurts)
  - IND -1.5 (Nelson and Buckner traded)
  - LAR, MIN, DET, WAS -1 each
  - SF +1
  
  Model margins are blended 80/20 with the market, and totals 85/15. Margin SD is 13.5, total SD 10, team-total SD 7.
- **Props:** decayed (0.8) game logs from nflverse, with a game-script factor of (implied team points / season ppg)^0.5. The model mean is 40% of the blended mean. Fair = market + 0.6 x (model - market), capped at ±6 points.
- **Joint probability:** a Gaussian copula with 400k simulations. A 50k-sim game model (margin and total to team points) loads each prop on team points or margin, using loadings estimated from 2026 data: QB yards ~ team points 0.31, WR receptions ~ QB yards 0.24, RB rush ~ margin 0.37, QB attempts ~ margin -0.19. That gives latent rho ~0.27 for Burrow and Higgins, 0 across games. A 400k-draw copula confirms the joints. The app's 20% same-game profit trim is applied to payouts.
- **Conservative re-pricing (sensitivity):** multiplicative de-vig, ladder rungs at raw/1.05, normal family. This lowers alt-rung props by 3-6 points and team legs by about 2.
  - Under it, the chosen parlays' joint probabilities would be about 0.64, 0.63 and 0.44, all still above the 0.55 and 0.42 targets.
  - A league-wide empirical check (a player's game relative to his leave-one-out mean) gave lower numbers again: about 0.69 for QBs clearing 80% of their average, 0.80 for RBs clearing 57%, and 0.72 for WRs clearing 64% of their receptions. That check is inflated by noise in the mean, so I treat it as a downside bound.
  - Bottom line: the true hit rates are probably a few points below the headline `prob`. This is why every leg sits on a role with 6-7 of 7 recent hits.
