## Model summary
- **Team lines.** Empirical 2015+ closing-line results from games.csv, anchored to Pinnacle's main line.
  - Spreads under 8: unshifted games within ±1 pt. Spreads of 8 or more: favorite-folded games within ±3 pts with integer shifts (new this run; see lessons).
  - Corrected in logit space toward Pinnacle's no-vig price at the nearest Pinnacle point. The model matches Pinnacle exactly at its points and runs 0.6-1.2 pts below the books' power de-vig median.
  - Market probability = 50% model + 50% median per-book power de-vig at the exact rung (2+ books).
- **Power shade.** Opponent-adjusted, recency-weighted EPA/play ratings (regressed 50%), rescaled ×1.5 to remove compression, with home field 1.5 (Mexico City 0).
  - Shift = 25% of the model-minus-market gap, capped at ±1 pt, halved for QB-change games (TB, MIN, NYJ, PHI, IND).
  - The prob effect is capped at ±2.5 pts.
- **Props.** Normal (QB yards), gamma with dud mass (rush/rec yards) or negative binomial (counts), fit to the Pinnacle main (weight 3), the DK main (2) and DK's power-de-vigged ladder (1).
  - Market = 50% fit + 50% median of the other books' per-rung de-vig.
  - Shade = 0.3 × (smoothed season hit rate − market), capped at -3/+1.5.
  - Calibration haircut -1.5 pts on every prop Over (held from Tuesday; there were no new graded rows to re-measure).
- **Parlays.** Every leg is in a different game (and different from the other legs in its parlay), so joint P is the product, with no same-game trim.
