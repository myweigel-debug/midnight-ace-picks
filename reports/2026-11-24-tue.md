## Model summary
- **Market baseline (changed this week):** de-vig each two-sided market proportionally. Use Pinnacle when it posts both sides at the exact line; otherwise take the median across the `cs` books (excluding DK and BetOnline) that post both sides. For Over-only ladders, take each book's implied price divided by its main-line overround, then the median. This is the same method the late-swap routine uses for `clp`, so this week's CLV is like-for-like. `cg` = the other books' median raw implied minus DK's implied (+ = DK cheaper).
- **Team sides:** the empirical margin distribution from games.csv (2012+), for games with a closing spread within ±1 point (±3, shifted by whole points, for spreads of 8+) and a closing total within ±3.5. Shade = 25% of (history - market), capped at ±1.5 pts.
  - Then a power shade: opponent-adjusted, recency-weighted (0.9/week) EPA/play ridge ratings from 2026 play-by-play, scaled to the market (fit: market margin = 0.86 x rating diff + 0.68). 25% of the model-market gap, capped at 1 pt of spread, converted with the empirical density, capped at ±2 prob pts and then **halved** (the overconfidence fix). Teams with a QB change or question get half of that again. The Rams/Packers get none (Nacua out, Parsons debut).
- **Props:** proportional consensus baseline, a **-2 pt haircut on Overs**, and a season hit-rate shade (0.3 x (smoothed hit rate - market), capped at -3/+1.5). Tuesday role filter: QBs with no injury designation; RBs with lead roles (60%+ snaps); WR/TE at 84%+ snaps over the last 3 weeks.
- **Parlays:** every leg is in a different game, so the joint probability is the product of the legs and there is no same-game trim. No leg or game is shared between parlays.
- **Sensitivity:** P x payout as published (final re-poll prices): [0.974, 0.962]. With **no** model shades (pure proportional market): [0.941, 0.948]. With only the line-history correction for team legs (actual hit rates run about 1-1.5 pts above proportional no-vig at -250..-649): about [0.96, 0.96]. These parlays are break-even at best and depend on the history shade, which is why both are $50 and why I didn't build a third.
