## Model summary
- **Team lines.** I fit the 2015+ closing-line residual distributions (margin, total, team score from games.csv) to each game's full Pinnacle ladder: the ML, main spread, and 13 alt spreads per side, 13 alt totals, and team totals. Then I corrected toward Pinnacle at each posted point and priced every DK rung.
  - My first kernel-on-spread version misfit big spreads. It had BUF at -14.5 effective against a market of -7.5, and GB +6.5 at 0.88 instead of 0.856. It also overrated alt-total unders by about 5 pts.
  - I caught it by comparing against the Pinnacle ladder and rebuilt it.
- **Power shading.** Opponent-adjusted EPA/play power ratings (recency-weighted, through Week 9), regressed 50%. The shade is 25% of the model-minus-market gap, capped at ±1.5 pts.
- **Props.** I fit negative-binomial or gamma distributions jointly to the Pinnacle or consensus-median no-vig main line and the DK ladder (with a free vig offset), then shaded with a 60/40 blend of market mean and recency-weighted log mean adjusted for the opponent's yards allowed to that position (regressed 50%).
  - The shade is capped at +2.5 / -6 pts, following last week's lesson.
  - Warren is held below his model value (0.800) because his snap share fell from 97% to 58% over the last 5 games as Rico Dowdle's role grew.
- **Parlays.** Each leg is in a different game, so the joint probability is the product of the leg probabilities. App payout is the product of decimal odds; there are no same-game groups.
