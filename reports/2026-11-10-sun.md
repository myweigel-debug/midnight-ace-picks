## Model summary
- **Props.** Market baseline = the median across books of each book's power-de-vigged price at the exact rung. Each book's exponent comes from its own two-way main line.
  - The model is a pooled 2025-26 "game stat / rolling 6-game mean" distribution, scaled to the market mean, which is solved from all two-sided main lines (Pinnacle weighted 3x).
  - That model runs too wide in absolute terms, so only its relative signal is used: (model − market − that family's median bias) × 0.3, capped at -3/+1.5 pts, minus 0.5 pt for alt-ladder hold, minus 1 pt if the player missed the rung in 2+ of his last 6 games.
  - Published fair is always within 3 pts of the no-vig market.
- **Game lines.**
  - Alt spreads: the empirical 2015+ distribution of favorite margins in games with closing spreads within 1 pt (no smoothing across key numbers), logit-shifted to Pinnacle's alt ladder.
  - Alt totals and team totals: the same approach with total and team-score residuals.
  - The fair value blends that 50/50 with the cross-book two-sided no-vig median at the same rung. This dropped BUF team total 25.5+ from a fake +4% EV to +0.7%.
- **Moneylines.** Pinnacle no-vig plus a situational check on 2010-25 closing lines (split 2010-17 / 2018-25).
  - Only "big favorite > 75%" beat the line in both halves (+1.1, +1.4 pts).
  - Divisional, road favorite, rest, bye, cold, wind and primetime effects did not replicate. Fresh injury news drives the remaining adjustments (±1.3 pts).
- **Fit-artifact check (lesson from Week 9).** I fit gamma (yards) or negative binomial (counts) distributions, with a 1.5-3% dud term, to each book's full ladder (main line plus alt rungs, power de-vigged). The medians across books came within ±1.5 pts of every published prop fair: Kelce 0.821 vs 0.817, Henry 0.793 vs 0.793, Lawrence 0.788 vs 0.784, Javonte 0.846 vs 0.845, Stafford 0.863 vs 0.870, Flournoy 0.773 vs 0.776, McMillan 0.881 vs 0.866, Chase Brown 0.810 vs 0.816, Herbert 0.831 vs 0.832, Pollard 0.795 vs 0.799, McBride 0.867 vs 0.857, Warren 0.893 vs 0.876. None of the edges is a fit artifact.
- **Joint probabilities.** Every core leg is in a different game, so legs are treated as independent. No same-game groups means no 0.8 trim.
- **Defense and kicker props** were priced by DK only (1-2 books), which fails the ≥3-book rule, so none were used.
